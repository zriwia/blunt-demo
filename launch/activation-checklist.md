# Morning Activation Checklist

## Minimum Go-Live Path

1. Confirm the X Ads account is unlocked and billable.
2. Confirm the landing page opens:
   - `https://zriwia.github.io/blunt-demo/paid/`
3. Confirm the survey opens:
   - `https://zriwia.github.io/blunt-demo/h10-survey.html`
4. Upload the three launch creatives:
   - `launch/creative/blunt_manifesto_15s.mp4`
   - `launch/creative/blunt_ad_square_01.png`
   - `launch/creative/blunt_ad_square_02.png`
5. Paste the matching tracked URLs from [`launch-plan-paid.md`](./launch-plan-paid.md).
6. Start with the simplest campaign structure possible.

## Campaign Setup

### Objective

- Website traffic / visits

### Creative Set

- 1 video creative
- 2 image creatives

### CTA

- Learn more
- Or equivalent low-friction website click CTA

## Copy To Pair With Creative

Use the copy pack in [`launch/ad-copy.md`](./ad-copy.md).

Fastest safe setup:

- Primary text 1 + headline 1 for video
- Primary text 2 + headline 2 for image 1
- Primary text 3 + headline 3 for image 2

## Tracking Checks

Before spending real money:

1. Open each tracked URL manually.
2. Click through to the survey.
3. Submit one test response.
4. Confirm the success state appears.

## First-Hour Monitoring

Watch these first:

- Are ads approved?
- Are clicks reaching `paid/`?
- Are people clicking into `h10-survey.html`?
- Are survey submissions coming in?

## If Something Breaks

### Ads Account Still Blocked

- Do not improvise the strategy
- Fix billing / Premium / account access first
- Keep the asset pack as-is

### Landing Loads But Video Does Not

- Launch anyway with image creatives first
- The video is a performance layer, not the core dependency

### Survey Gets Clicks But No Submits

- Read the qualitative responses that do arrive
- Consider shortening copy on the landing or reducing perceived effort

## Definition Of Ready

Tomorrow morning is a go if these are true:

- Ads account can spend
- `paid/` loads
- `h10-survey.html` loads
- One test submission works
- Three creatives are uploadable
- Tracked URLs are pasted correctly
