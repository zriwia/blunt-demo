# Paid Ad Copy Pack

## Messaging Spine

Blunt is not selling “better AI answers.”
Blunt is selling **visible confidence**.

That means the copy should sound sharp, skeptical, and clean.
Avoid inflated launch language.

## Primary Text Variants

### 1. Thesis-Led

AI should not just answer. It should show how sure it is.  
Blunt makes confidence visible so people know when to trust, when to verify, and when to push back.

### 2. Trust-Gap

The problem with AI is not only accuracy. It is fake certainty.  
Blunt makes uncertainty visible instead of hiding it in polished wording.

### 3. Product Framing

What if AI showed its confidence as part of the interface?  
Blunt is testing whether visible confidence makes AI feel more honest, trustworthy, and useful.

### 4. Consequence Framing

When people act on AI output, tone is not enough.  
Blunt shows confidence explicitly so users can judge when to rely on an answer and when to double-check it.

### 5. Curiosity Hook

Most AI sounds confident even when it should not.  
Blunt explores a different model: show the confidence layer openly and let the user decide what to trust.

## Headlines

2. The AI that will not pretend
